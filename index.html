FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers before you get started.

Is Exalyte free to use?

Creating an account is free and needs no credit card. Some batches and premium exams may have a fee, which is always shown on the Batches page before you enrol.

How do live exams work?

A live exam runs in a fixed window with a precision countdown timer, and your answers are submitted automatically when time is up.

Can I retry an exam?

Yes. Practice mode lets you retry a question set without time pressure and review the explanations afterwards.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Exalyte is designed to work on phones, tablets and computers.

Where can I get help?
