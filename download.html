    Before you share

    • In Drive, set the file to Anyone with the link. Otherwise people will see a "request access" page.
    • Works with PDFs, images, ZIPs and other files. For a Google Doc, Sheet or Slides file, download it as a PDF first and upload that PDF to Drive.
    • Tell students to open the link in Google Chrome. The share page also asks them to, and opens Chrome for them where the phone allows it.
    • Folders can't be downloaded this way. Share the individual files instead.
